## 2. What we process
- **Account** — email, name, role, language, timezone, password hash or Google account link.
- **Profiles** — the player card (level, style, experience) and the coach profile (bio, languages, services, venue, avatar).
- **Videos and annotations** — the clips you upload, their transcoded versions, and the coach's annotations made during sessions.
- **Sessions** — bookings, times, who joined the video room and when, confirmations, cancellations and reschedules.
- **Payments** — amounts, currency, status, provider references and refunds; no card numbers reach our servers.
- **Disputes and reviews** — the texts you write and the outcome.
- **Verification calls** — the booking of a coach's verification call and its outcome.
- **Legal acceptances** — which version of which document you accepted, when and in which language.

## 5. Cookies and tracking
With your consent we record the pages you open, the steps of a booking, errors on the page and a replay of some sessions in PostHog Cloud (EU). Everything you type is masked in replays; links containing codes or tokens are stripped before anything leaves your browser. Bookings, payments and disputes are counted on our servers regardless of your choice, because they mirror records we keep to run the service. You can change your choice at any time under "Cookie settings" in the footer.
